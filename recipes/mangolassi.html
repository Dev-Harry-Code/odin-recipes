Mango Lassi

Description

Mango Lassi is a popular Indian drink made from ripe mangoes, yogurt, and spices.

It is a refreshing and creamy drink that is perfect for hot weather.

Ingredients

  1. 2 ripe mangoes
  2. 1 cup yogurt
  3. 1/2 cup milk
  4. 1 tablespoon sugar
  5. 1/2 teaspoon cardamom powder

Steps

  • Peel and chop the mangoes.
  • In a blender, combine the chopped mangoes, yogurt, milk, sugar, and cardamom powder.
  • Blend until smooth and creamy.
  • Pour into glasses and serve chilled.
