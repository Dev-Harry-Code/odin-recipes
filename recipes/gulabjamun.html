Gulab Jamun

Description

Gulab Jamun is a popular Indian dessert made from milk solids and soaked in a sugar syrup.

It is a sweet and indulgent treat that is often served during special occasions and festivals.

Ingredients

  1. 1 cup milk powder
  2. 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  3. 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  4. 2 tablespoons ghee (clarified butter)
  5. 1/4 cup milk (or as needed)
  6. Oil or ghee for deep frying
  7. 1 1/2 cups sugar
  8. 1 1/2 cups water
  9. 4-5 cardamom pods, crushed
  10. Rose water or saffron strands (optional)

Steps

  • In a mixing bowl, combine milk powder, all-purpose flour, and baking soda. Add ghee and mix well until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  • Add milk gradually and knead the mixture into a smooth dough. Cover and let it rest for 10-15 minutes.
  • In a separate pan, prepare the sugar syrup by combining sugar, water, and crushed cardamom pods. Bring it to a boil and simmer for 5-7 minutes until slightly thickened. Add rose water or saffron if desired. Keep the syrup warm.
  • Divide the dough into small portions and roll them into smooth balls, ensuring there are no cracks.
  • Heat oil or ghee in a deep frying pan over low to medium heat. Fry the balls in batches until they turn golden brown. Remove and drain on paper towels.
  • While the fried balls are still warm, immerse them in the warm sugar syrup. Let them soak for at least 30 minutes before serving.
  • Serve the Gulab Jamun warm or at room temperature, garnished with chopped nuts if desired.
