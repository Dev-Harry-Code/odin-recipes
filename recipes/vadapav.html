Vada Pav

Description

Vada Pav is a popular Indian street food made from a deep-fried potato patty served in a soft bun.

It is a delicious and satisfying snack that is perfect for any time of the day.

Ingredients

  1. 3 to 4 medium potatoes, peeled and mashed
  2. 2 to 3 green chilies
  3. 1-inch ginger, crushed or grated
  4. 5 to 6 garlic cloves, crushed or made into a paste
  5. 1 tablespoon oil
  6. 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
  7. Pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  8. 8 to 10 fresh curry leaves
  9. 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  10. Salt to taste
  11. Chopped fresh coriander leaves
  12. 1 to 1.5 cups gram flour (besan)
  13. Pinch of carom seeds (ajwain) or extra hing
  14. Tiny pinch of baking soda (optional)
  15. Soft white dinner rolls or pav
  16. Fresh coriander-mint chutney
  17. Dry garlic chutney
  18. Deep-fried salted green chilies (optional)

Steps

  • Boil the potatoes, peel them, and mash them in a bowl.
  • In a pan, heat oil and add mustard seeds, asafoetida, and curry leaves. Sauté for a few seconds.
  • Add the green chilies, ginger, and garlic paste. Sauté for a minute.
  • Add turmeric powder and salt. Mix well.
  • Add the mashed potatoes and chopped coriander leaves. Mix everything together and cook for a few minutes. Let the mixture cool.
  • Once cooled, shape the potato mixture into small round patties.
  • In a separate bowl, prepare the gram flour batter by mixing gram flour, carom seeds, baking soda, and water to make a smooth batter.
  • Heat oil in a deep frying pan. Dip each potato patty into the gram flour batter and deep-fry until golden brown. Remove and drain on paper towels.
  • Slice the pav horizontally, keeping one edge intact. Spread coriander-mint chutney and dry garlic chutney on the inner sides of the pav. Place a fried potato patty in between and serve hot with deep-fried salted green chilies if desired.
